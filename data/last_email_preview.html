Florida: Two New Marijuana Legalization Initiatives Filed for 2028
Legislation / The Marijuana Herald / 2026-10-02
Two new proposed constitutional amendments to legalize adult-use marijuana were filed with Florida officials this week for the 2028 ballot. One was approved by the Division of Elections on September 30 and the other is awaiting a financial impact review; both would let existing medical dispensaries sell to adults 21 and older, and both are separate from Smart & Safe Florida's older initiative, which the state now lists for 2028. For patients, adult-use legalization is no longer possible before 2028, and the proposals would change how the market is licensed and who can sell.
IRS Whistleblower Office Reviewing Claim Over $10 Million Florida Money Trail Tied to Anti-Marijuana Campaign
Social / The Marijuana Herald / 2026-10-02
The IRS Whistleblower Office is reviewing a claim filed by Florida Rep. Alex Andrade about $10 million in state Medicaid settlement money that ended up funding opposition to the 2024 marijuana legalization amendment. It matters to patients and advocates because it keeps the question of how that campaign was funded under federal scrutiny, though a review is not a finding of wrongdoing.
